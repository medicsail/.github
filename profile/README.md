MedicSail is a healthcare licensing consultancy. We help internationally trained doctors, nurses,
midwives, dentists, pharmacists and allied health professionals get registered and working in the UK,
Ireland, Europe and the USA: eligibility assessment, route planning, registration applications, credential
verification, exam preparation, career guidance and relocation guidance. We also publish free guides and
tools, checked against the official sources, so anyone can understand their route before they start.

## How we help

- **Eligibility assessment:** which countries and regulators you can register with, and the realistic route.
- **Registration support:** preparing and checking applications to the GMC, NMC, HCPC, NMBI, the Medical
  Council, CORU, German state authorities and US state boards.
- **Credential verification:** EPIC, primary source verification and good standing, step by step.
- **Exam preparation:** PLAB, NMC CBT and OSCE, NCLEX, USMLE, the ORE and the Kenntnisprüfung.
- **Career and relocation guidance:** sponsoring employers, pay, visas and the first weeks in a new country.

Start with a free eligibility check at [medicsail.com](https://medicsail.com/contact?service=eligibility-assessment).

## What our free guides cover

- **Registration and licensing** with the regulators: GMC, NMC, HCPC, GDC and GPhC in the UK, the
  Medical Council, NMBI and CORU in Ireland, the Approbation and recognition bodies across Europe, and
  the state boards, ECFMG and NCSBN routes in the USA.
- **Licensing exams:** PLAB, the NMC CBT and OSCE, NCLEX, USMLE, the ORE, the Kenntnisprüfung and more.
- **Visas and moving:** the Health and Care Worker visa, Irish employment permits, the EU Blue Card and
  US routes, with what they cost and how long they take.
- **Jobs and pay:** hospitals that hire internationally, NHS bands, and what you take home.
- **A glossary** of the bodies, portals, permits and ID numbers you meet on the way, country by country.
- **Free tools:** visa cost and salary calculators, an English score checker, a route finder and
  registration timelines.

## Who it is for

Health professionals trained outside the country they want to work in, who need to know which route
applies to them, what it costs, how long it takes and what to do next.

## Principles

- **Official sources only.** Every fee, deadline and rule comes from the regulator, government or
  agency that sets it, and each page lists its sources and when it was last checked.
- **Plain English.** No jargon without an explanation.
- **Free to read.** The guides are free, with no sign-up.
- **Honest advice.** We tell you when a route will not work for you. We are not a regulator or a recruiter.
